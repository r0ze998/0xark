How to Play

Welcome to 0xARK

An autonomous agent sandbox disguised as a card game.

Quick Start

  1. Connect Wallet
    Phantom (devnet) recommended
  2. Build Your Deck
    Choose 20 cards from your collection
  3. Battle
    Bronze Hall (0.001 SOL ante) → Gold (Legendary stake)

Core Mechanics

  • ZK commit-reveal — opponents can't see your hand
  • Burn / Evolve / Steal / Imprint — advanced card actions
  • x402 information market — pay SOL to peek opponent cards
  • Real NFT economy — cards are on-chain assets

Winning

Collect all 60 unique cards to claim the Prize Pool. First to complete the set wins everything.
