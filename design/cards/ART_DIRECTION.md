# 0xARK — sixty faces of the Drowned Archive

Art direction, 2026-09-07. This extends the current `DESIGN.md` and the active
six-faction catalog. The retired five-clan `LORE_SHARDS.md` is not source canon
for this set. New visual situations below are production proposals, not added
game mechanics or a rewrite of card identities.

## Shared world

An ancient coastal archive survives above a violent, moonlit sea. Its inhabitants
guard, exchange, steal, study, contemplate and rebuild what the water has not yet
claimed. Black-green stone, worn brass, salt, vellum, old cloth and seafoam light
connect every image. Small signs of custody — a seal, repair, clasp or notch —
suggest objects with a history. Avoid crypto logos, coins with modern brands,
floating interface symbols, neon science fiction and generic treasure piles.

Retain the existing portraits' romantic illustrated fantasy character language:
precise expressive ink contours, sculpted hair, readable faces, ornamental metal,
restrained painterly shadows and tactile surfaces. Place those characters inside
the Archive's atmospheric world. No photographic faces, plastic 3D or pixel art.
Legendary faces retain their established principal visual identities.

## Faction grammar

| Faction | Material and accent | Shape and occupation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knight | Blue steel, ivory, aged brass | Shield arches, upright weight, guardians |
| Merchant | Ochre leather, brass, parchment | Balances, seal discs, layered clothing |
| Pirate | Oxidized crimson, tarred black, rope | Triangles, asymmetry, forward motion |
| Scholar | Muted violet, indigo, vellum | Circles, lenses, observatory instruments |
| Monk | Seafoam, sage, ivory cloth, prayer stone | Grounded curves, open space, stillness |
| Engineer | Copper, amber, graphite, seaworn iron | Braces, calipers, tidal machinery |

Common cards are concrete people, work or expendable artifacts. Uncommon cards
show specialized equipment and responsibility. Merge-only cards visibly inherit
one distinct motif from each of their two source cards. Rare cards change the
visual scale or imply influence over other members. Legendary cards have the
clearest iconic silhouette and a unique setting, rather than more visual noise.
Rarity changes narrative scope, not illustration quality.

## Asset rules

- One distinct illustration per actual ID, with no repeated faction portrait.
- Individual square masters; one main subject and a strong light/dark silhouette.
- Face or principal artifact and identifying tool grouped within the central 70%
  and upper two-thirds, legible in the existing 140px card portrait window.
- Background supports the occupation but remains lower contrast than the subject.
- No baked frame, card name, typography, number, stat or action icon. The existing
  renderer supplies exact catalog information and player-selected actions.
- Burn-capable cards may show an expended object; do not depict a person burning
  or invent a new action. Merge lineage is visual, not a new recipe.
- Across each ten-card faction vary age, face, hair, pose, silhouette and focal
  object. Distinguish neighboring IDs even in monochrome and thumbnail crops.
- Preserve the original six portraits and create new sibling files.

## Production record

`briefs-01-20.json`, `briefs-21-40.json`, `briefs-41-60.json` define all subjects.
Image production uses the built-in image generator with this shared direction
and a separate per-card brief. Completion and visual inspection are recorded
per image; a brief alone is not a completed illustration.
