## What are Lore Shards?

Every card in 0xARK has **three Lore Shards** — fragments of story that reveal the card's history, one piece at a time.

- **Shard 1** — Witness perspective. Unlocked the moment you acquire the card. This is what people saw.
- **Shard 2** — Deck participation. Unlocked after playing with this card in your deck under Clan-specific conditions. This is what the card has done.
- **Shard 3** — The secret. Unlocked via Gold Hall duel participation or x402 purchase (price = 5% of the card's current market value). This is what the card truly is.

For Season 1 Frontier Hackathon submission: **all 60 cards have Shard 1 drafted**. **15 key cards have Shards 2 and 3** (the 4 Legendaries, the 6 Rares, and 1 representative Uncommon per Clan). Remaining 45 cards have "Shard coming soon" placeholder for Shards 2 and 3 — to be drafted Season 2.

## Black Flag

**Clan motto:** *"The sea rejects kings. So do we."*  
**Shard 2 trigger for Black Flag cards:** Use card in 3 winning duels.

**Shard 1:** *"Every port has them. Nobody asks where they came from. They don't answer. They watch the sailors drunk in the tavern. They watch the sailors' coin purses. They are watching you now."*

**Shard 1:** *"He misses nothing. Not ships, not throats, not debts. Debts especially. Three clans owe him money, and he has not forgotten the amounts."*

### 4. Salt-Bitten Deckhand

### 5. Powder Monkey

**Shard 1:** *"He was eight when the navy took his father. He was ten when he took the navy's ship. He is fourteen now. He has been paid, and paid well, for four years. He does not know what 'childhood' means."*

### 6. Reef Pilot

**Shard 1:** *"No map shows these waters. The maps are in her head. She will not share. When pressed, she has been known to sail passengers into the rocks and swim away. She does not apologize."*

### 31. First Mate Kaelith

**Shard 1:** *"Second in command of the ghost fleet. The captain answers to her, though he would deny it. She does not correct him. She does not need to."*

**Shard 2 (unlocked after 3 wins with this card in deck):** *"The crew count their shares before each raid. Kaelith counts them after. No raid has ever come up short. No crew member has ever asked why."*

**Shard 3 (unlocked via Gold Hall or x402):** *"She was the navigator of the Calypso, the royal flagship, the night it 'sank.' She is still that navigator. The Calypso is still sailing. She has not told the crew this. They suspect, but nobody asks."*

### 32. Cannon Captain

**Shard 1:** *"He lost an eye, an ear, three fingers. He keeps firing. The crew calls him luck. He calls it arithmetic. Every shot takes something. He has enough left for 47 more shots. He has counted."*

### 33. Bloodflag Corsair

**Shard 1:** *"Crimson sail, crimson deck, crimson hands. He only sails toward the royal navy. He has never returned from a raid without his sail even redder than before. The crew no longer asks whose blood."*

### 34. Mutineer

**Shard 1:** *"He speaks softly to men who follow orders. By morning, those men follow him. He has overthrown four captains. The fifth one cannot be overthrown because the fifth one is him."*

### 51. Ghost Fleet Captain

**Shard 1:** *"His ship was sunk by the royal navy seven years ago. His ship still sails. Nobody has explained. Salvage divers report a hull shape on the seabed, matching the Calypso exactly. But the hull is empty. And the Calypso sails tomorrow."*

**Shard 2 (unlocked after 3 wins with this card in deck):** *"The Ghost Fleet has raided eleven royal supply ships. Each time, the boarding crew reports the same thing: the royal officers surrender before any blow is struck. Some kneel. None speak of why."*

**Shard 3 (unlocked via Gold Hall or x402):** *"Seven years ago, on the night Elyon fell, the Calypso was ordered to dock at the royal capital for the king's protection. The captain refused the order. The king died that night. The navy sank the Calypso in retaliation. Or tried to. The captain has never sailed without a compass pointing toward the fallen throne. He has not yet reached it. He is nearly there."*

## Neutral / Events

### 54. The Prince in Exile

**Shard 1:** *"Some say the heir lived. Some say the heir is among us. Some are paid well to say nothing. A boy with Valerius's eyes was seen in a border village, age 18, this spring. He was seen by exactly one farmer, who has since been paid enough to forget."*

**Shard 2 (unlocked after deck containing cards from 4+ clans wins 3 duels):** *"The village has changed hands three times in the last six months. Each new lord has razed different buildings. None of them have razed the mill where the boy was said to sleep. This is coincidence, the lords insist. They will not meet one another's eyes when they say this."*

**Shard 3 (unlocked via Gold Hall or x402):** *"The boy in the village is not the prince. The boy knows this. The prince — the real prince — is much closer than any of the five clans suspect, and works a job so ordinary that no one would believe it. The boy in the village is bait. He volunteered. He does not know why the prince chose him, but he suspects the prince looks nothing like the portrait and needed a decoy with the right eyes. The boy has the right eyes. He hopes this will be enough."*

### 55. The Assassin's Letter

**Shard 1:** *"Fifteen years ago, someone wrote this. The writing has not aged. The ink is still wet. The paper tested 15 years old by every method. The ink tested fresh. Two of the three scholars consulted have since vanished."*

But the letter's date is last Tuesday. The letter has been in continuous existence the whole time, despite never having existed before. Scholars argue. The letter is not listening."*

**Shard 3 (unlocked via Gold Hall or x402):** *"The letter is written in the handwriting of whoever is holding it. Each new holder sees their own script on the paper. The name of the target has changed, too — it is whoever the current holder most fears. The original target, fifteen years ago, was Valerius. The current target, if you are holding this card, is yourself."*

### 56. The Kingdom's Forgotten Oath

**Shard 1:** *"A promise older than the throne. A promise nobody remembers making. A promise that cannot be broken. When spoken aloud, men grip their weapons without knowing why. When written down, the ink refuses to dry."*

The oath has never been publicly recited, because nobody remembers the words. Clansfolk grip their weapons regardless. The weapons, too, remember."*

**Shard 3 (unlocked via Gold Hall or x402):** *"The power to whom the oath was sworn has not been named because naming it breaks it. This is the one detail the first clan leaders agreed upon. It is the one detail every generation since has inherited but forgotten. The oath still holds. The thing it binds still watches. The card in your hand has been watching you for fifteen years."*

## Legendaries

Each Legendary represents one clan's answer to *"what is a king?"*

### 57. Sceptre of Valerius

**Clan:** Hollow Blade | **Philosophy:** Might — *the one who can defeat the king is king.*

**Shard 1:** *"The Wise-King's sceptre was shattered the night he died. The fragments remember. They are still looking for a hand. Seven fragments were recovered from the throne room. Twelve fragments are on display in various halls. Nobody has explained this."*

**Shard 2 (unlocked after this card KOs 5 opposing characters):** *"Each time the Sceptre transfers ownership — each duel where the holder loses — the Sceptre re-assembles itself slightly differently. The shape is the same. The precise arrangement of fragments is not. No holder has held the same Sceptre. Each holder has held the next version of it."*

**Shard 3 (unlocked via Gold Hall or x402):** *"The Sceptre is alive. Not metaphorically. It chose Valerius. When Valerius died, it did not break — it rejected the body. It has been choosing holders ever since, preferring those who win its duels honestly. Every holder who has lost the Sceptre in Gold Hall has felt, in the moment of loss, a small relief, as if the Sceptre had already forgiven them. Most do not notice. A few do, and sleep better afterward, and never speak of it."*

## Writing notes (for draft review)

**Shard 1 voice**: Witness perspective, external. Things people have *seen* or *heard* about the card. Never first-person. Short sentences, vivid images.

**Shard 2 voice**: Partial insider. What the card has *done*. Tells a small story that hints at the card's agency or history.

**Shard 3 voice**: The true secret. Often reveals that the card has an agenda or awareness the world hasn't noticed. Should feel like a shiver when read. Shorter is better.

**Style throughout**: dark fantasy, fragmentary, like medieval chronicles or Borges. No melodrama. The weight comes from what is not said.

**Consistency rule**: No card should contradict another card's Shard. The lore is shared. If the Sceptre chose Valerius (Sceptre Shard 3) and the Crown chose Valerius (Crown Shard 3), then both are true — Valerius was chosen twice. This is meaningful.
